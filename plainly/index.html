Plainly

A calm, simple personal finance app that we build for our own family. It is not a commercial product: it is not for sale, it is not listed in any app store, and we do not take on new users.

Private · invitation only Android No ads, no tracking Data hosted in the EU

Plainly is an internal, personal product. Only members of one family, invited by Puredata OÜ, can install it, through a closed Google Play testing track. There is no public download, sign-up or price.

When the family connects a bank account, Plainly gets read-only access to that account's balance and transactions through Enable Banking, a licensed account information service provider. Plainly can never make payments or move money.

What it does

  • BudgetsMonthly budgets by category, and what's still safe to spend.
  • TransactionsEvery payment in one place, searchable by month, category or account.
  • Goals and billsSavings goals, and the recurring bills coming up next.
  • HouseholdShared accounts, budgets and goals between family members.
  • Bank syncNew payments from the family's own bank accounts, brought in automatically and read-only.
  • App lockA PIN or fingerprint in front of the app; data encrypted on the phone.

How Plainly treats your data

  • Everything lives first in an encrypted database on your phone. Without an account, nothing leaves it.
  • With an account, a copy syncs to our database in Frankfurt (EU), so the family's phones stay in step. Only you, and the household members you share with, can read it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share data with anyone outside the processors named in the privacy policy.
