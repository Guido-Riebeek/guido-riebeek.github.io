Plainly  ·  Account management

Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your Plainly account and all its data at any time. Plainly is a private app by Puredata OÜ, registered in Estonia, EU. We follow the GDPR and complete every deletion request within 30 days.

How to request deletion

1
Optional, in the app first: export a copy of your data (Profile → Export My Data) and disconnect your bank (Profile → Bank Sync → Disconnect). If you don't, we end the bank connection for you.
2
Email info@puredata.ee with the subject Account deletion request — Plainly, from or mentioning the email address of your Plainly account.
3
We confirm receipt and delete your account within 30 days, then email you once it's done.

What happens to your data

DataOutcome
Account (email, password, Google sign-in link) Deleted within 30 days
Cloud copy of your finance data (accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, bills, categories) Deleted within 30 days
Bank connection and fetched bank data Bank approval ended and data deleted within 30 days
Household membership You are removed from your household
Data on your phone Stays on your phone until you uninstall Plainly or clear its storage
Purchase records from early test versions Held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies

Plainly has no subscriptions or paid features, so there is nothing to cancel. Questions? See the Plainly Privacy Policy or email info@puredata.ee.
