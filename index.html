About us

A small studio with a clear focus

Puredata OÜ is a software company registered in Estonia. We design and develop mobile applications that solve everyday problems with clarity and restraint — no unnecessary complexity, no data harvesting, no bloat.

Privacy first
Your data belongs to you. We collect only what is strictly necessary to make the product work, and we never sell it.
Purposeful design
Every screen, every interaction is considered. We build less, but better.
Independent
No investors, no growth-at-all-costs mindset. We build for users, not metrics.
